02 About

Editing is the
final rewrite.

Ten years in the editing bay — three of them as an in-house editor at Aatpat Productions. I treat editing not as a technical afterthought, but as the final rewrite of the screenplay: stories are built in the cut, rhythm first.

Whether working on feature-length narratives, OTT series, or short-form brand campaigns, the approach balances story rhythm, visual polish, and emotional intent.
